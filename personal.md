## __Kids!__
Because I enjoy being around kids, summer 2017 I decided to work part-time at Margaret's House. It's RIT's Day Care for little kids. While I enjoyed playing with kids there, I was given an opportunity to represent India as part of their Kids on Campus Program. It's a program for kids between ages 8-12 where they are taught various things. As part of this, I shared India's culture, history and our way of living life everyday. Look [__here.__](https://www.flickr.com/photos/159618694@N08/albums/72157669226813777) to see what these amazing little kids gave me the next day I went to school :). I loved every bit of this summer.
